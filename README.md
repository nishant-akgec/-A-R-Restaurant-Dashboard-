# 🍽️ A.R Restaurant — Power BI Dashboard

**Turning Restaurant Data into Meaningful Business Insights**

## 📌 Project Overview

How can a restaurant owner understand their business performance without manually analysing multiple records?

Imagine a restaurant owner in Bulandshahr who runs a restaurant but doesn't have a clear, consolidated view of sales, popular menu items, customer patterns, and overall business performance.

This project demonstrates how **Power BI and data visualisation** can help address these challenges by transforming raw data into an interactive dashboard.

## 🎯 Business Problem

A restaurant owner may struggle to answer important questions:

- Which menu categories generate the most sales?
- Which food items are the most popular?
- How do sales change from month to month?
- Which payment methods do customers use?
- Where are customers located?
- How do dine-in and takeaway orders compare?
- What do key performance indicators reveal about business performance?

Without a consolidated dashboard, finding these answers can take time and make decision-making more difficult.

## 💡 My Solution

I created an interactive Power BI dashboard that brings key restaurant performance metrics and visualisations together in one place.

### 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Sales:** Overview of sales generated.
- **Total Orders:** Number of orders processed.
- **Contribution Profit:** Contribution profit recorded in the demo dataset.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Average sales value per order.

### 📈 Dashboard Visualisations

- **Sales by Category:** Compare sales across menu categories.
- **Sales by Food Item:** Explore the sales contribution of individual menu items.
- **Monthly Sales Trend:** Analyse changes in sales across months and years.
- **Payment Method Analysis:** Understand the distribution of payment methods.
- **Customer Location Map:** Explore the geographic distribution of customers.
- **Order Type Analysis:** Compare dine-in and takeaway orders using an interactive slicer.

### 🔍 Potential Business Insights

With real restaurant data, this dashboard could help investigate:

1. Which categories contribute most to total sales.
2. Which menu items may deserve more attention in menu planning.
3. Whether sales show seasonal or monthly patterns.
4. Which payment methods are most commonly used.
5. Which customer locations may offer opportunities for targeted promotions.
6. How dine-in and takeaway performance differs.

These are potential analyses, not verified findings from a real restaurant.
